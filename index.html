How it works

See everything. Act on anything.

Forge sits on your site and watches the full player journey. Every signal feeds a profile that sharpens over time. Every action pushes the player where you want them.

1 · SEE

Every action, tracked

Forge tracks every action a player takes. Pages, clicks, bets, deposits, cashier opens, exits. The full journey, not just what your platform reports.

2 · LEARN

A profile that sharpens

Every visit builds the picture. Current state, playing habits, preferences. Forge learns each player and gets more accurate over time.

3 · PERSONALIZE

The right nudge, right now

Based on what it learned, Forge picks the most suitable action for this player at this moment. Not a generic banner everyone sees. A nudge shaped by what it knows about them.

4 · DIRECT

Toward your chosen outcome

Every nudge moves the player toward an outcome you set. Sign up, deposit, play, come back. You pick the destination.
